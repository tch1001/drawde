Phase 2 · the viewer new

Our own viewer built on EmbedPDF. R region mode — drag a box around an equation and the high-DPI crop lands in the right panel. T text mode. Hold Shift to add to the selection instead of replacing it; box and text selections mix freely. Each box persists on the page with a × to remove it.
